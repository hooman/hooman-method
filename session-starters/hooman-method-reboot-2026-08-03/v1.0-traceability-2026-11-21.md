## Clean-room critique of the v1.0 text (step 6 of PR #5)

An independent agent read only SKILL.md, `checkpoint.md`, `project.md`, the design session, A3,
A8, and this table. It could not write. Every point below was checked against the text and
accepted. Changes made:

1. **Making things public:**
   - decisions.1 now exempts the tranche's own branch and draft PR. Issues, comments, releases,
     and settings stay one-way calls.
   - proj.8 issues now need the principal's clearance.
   - This resolves a literal conflict with tranche.1 in public repositories.
2. **Out-of-scope edits for negative controls:** verify.3 now needs the envelope's **Authority**
   field to name them. They are never committed, and are reverted before the next push.
   Otherwise they are a stop condition. The earlier default granted autonomy that no call
   cleared.
3. **entry.3 (new):** at re-entry, list what waits on the principal, oldest first with its age.
   This covers the stale PR queue, and next-action lines that exist only on a branch.
4. **context.3 (new):** don't-touch defaults in every stance. This was the costliest drop.
5. **records.2:** the next-action line is rewritten at each checkpoint, not only at close,
   because the session may end without one. **records.3:** covers stale records merely routed
   around.
6. **rec.1:** says what to do when the named revision is not installed.
7. **decisions.4:** covers repositories with no remote review, and the project commit guard.
8. **Overreach toned down:**
   - the claims from A3 and A8 in `project.md` and `checkpoint.md` now state their limits
   - cp.3 is now an instruction, not a claim
   - proj.7 is now a set of options, not requirements

**Still open after the critique:**
- every subject was the same model (Opus 5.5); v1.0 is untested with another vendor's model
- a cut in the middle of a push is untested
- no v1.0 entry rule has been exercised, because A3 ran under the v0.8 workspace `AGENTS.md`
- "Git and code" in practice means "GitHub with remote review"
